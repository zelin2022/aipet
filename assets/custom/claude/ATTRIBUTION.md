# Claude pose adaptations

These are AI-assisted new-pose adaptations of the CrossPet Claude character, generated for Aipet's private, noncommercial use. They are separate from the unchanged imported artwork.

Original artwork/design source: [CrossPet by lokicorvus and contributors](https://github.com/lokicorvus/crosspet), pinned to commit `6e98970f6ff9b44e8ac85215241cabcd78f67d92`. CrossPet credits a community Claude personification and references [ai-school-op](https://github.com/lshhhhhhh/ai-school-op) and [openpet-ai-girls](https://github.com/AwesomeHou/openpet-ai-girls).

`wave-v1.png` was made with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool using the original Claude idle, happy and morning-greeting poses as visual references. Modification: a waving greeting pose with the original book held to one side. It is registered for Aipet's greeting pose pool. The generator's PNG bytes and alpha channel are preserved, with its prompt and provenance alongside it.

The additional approved pose filenames are `thinking-curious-v1.png` (curious thinking), `thinking-focused-v1.png` (focused thinking), `pat-lean-v1.png` (leaning into petting), `pat-content-v1.png` (contented petting response), `coaxed-affection-v1.png` (affectionate attention-seeking) and `hairplay-v1.png` (playing with a hair strand). Each generated adaptation records its own prompt, references, modification, image hash and dimensions in the accompanying provenance. These illustrations preserve the original character design while changing the pose and expression. Only files present in the verified custom manifest are included in the application.

The work-activity adaptations are `reading-studious-v1.png` (studious reading), `reading-page-turn-v1.png` (turning a page), `coding-quill-v1.png` (writing with a quill), `coding-review-v1.png` (reviewing written work), `tool-inspect-v1.png` (inspecting a tool) and `tool-adjust-v1.png` (adjusting a tool). These use the same character and costume references, changing their pose, expression and hand-held props to support Aipet's reading, coding and running-tool states. Their generated PNG bytes and alpha channels are preserved, with separate prompts and provenance beside each image.

The social adaptations are `aloof-look-away-v1.png` (crossed arms and looking away), `wink-v1.png` (a direct, playful wink) and `black-tea-v1.png` (sipping black tea from a tiny cup). Their expressions, gestures and tea props were generated against the original character and approved same-character adaptations. These changes preserve her established design and have individual prompts, source references, output hashes, alpha analysis and display-framing records. The original proud and puffed-cheek illustrations are reused without modification for smug and attention-seeking reactions.
