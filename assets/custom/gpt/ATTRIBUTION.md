# GPT pose redesign and adaptations

These are AI-assisted adaptations of the existing CrossPet GPT/ChatGPT white-dragon character for Aipet's private, noncommercial use. They are separate from the unchanged imported artwork. The selected redesign keeps her pearl-white hair, violet eyes, dragon features and white/silver interwoven knot ornament while adding amethyst horns, stronger lavender hair shadows, a deeper violet dress and clearer separation between hair, clothing, hands, wings and tail.

Original artwork/design source: [CrossPet by lokicorvus and contributors](https://github.com/lokicorvus/crosspet), pinned to commit `6e98970f6ff9b44e8ac85215241cabcd78f67d92`. The initial `idle-v1.png` preserves the approved `design/concepts/gpt/idle-redesign-v4.png` bytes. Its accompanying record identifies the concept and reference hashes. The other generated images use that approved character, costume, palette and ornament as references, while adapting the pose, expression and any hand-held props for Aipet's presentation states. Generation uses OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Each image has a separate exact prompt and provenance record with references, modification, output hash, dimensions and transparent-background request. The generator's PNG bytes and native alpha are retained without recompression or pixel edits; framing metadata describes display bounds only.

The complete profile includes three idle poses, four thinking poses, three reading poses, three coding poses, three running-tool poses, one success pose, one error pose, two waiting poses, one sleeping pose, four petting responses, one poking response, humming, stretching, yawning, four greetings, two affectionate idle expressions, puffed cheeks, hair-play, and a separate matching idle blink. These are alternate still illustrations with independently implemented motion and timing, rather than a pre-timed animation sequence.

Staged files are not included in the application. Only images and documents listed in the verified custom manifest under an explicitly enabled, complete GPT replacement profile become runtime assets. The original GPT artwork, licenses and provenance remain unchanged.

The subsequent social adaptations are `aloof-look-away-v1.png` (crossed arms and looking away), `smug-v1.png` (a self-satisfied expression) and `white-monster-v1.png` (sipping a white Monster energy-drink can with a dragon-claw emblem). They preserve the approved violet costume, amethyst horns and white/silver knot while changing gestures, expressions and the hand-held drink prop. The depicted drink brand is a user-requested visual detail and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement. Each adaptation retains its exact prompt, source references, output hash and alpha-framing provenance. The already-winking morning greeting is reused as the wink action through a manifest alias; its PNG and provenance remain unchanged. The existing puffed-cheek image is reused for the attention-timer reaction.
